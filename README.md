# Mowing Contracts Plus

A single-player mod for Farming Simulator 25 by **Boy Meets Farming**.

## Features

New mowing contracts have a **25% chance** of offering you the grass in exchange for a **50% reduction in the contract reward**. Equipment hire costs still apply.

Accepting the offer allows you to:
- Ted the grass into hay.
- Windrow grass or hay.
- Bale it.
- Collect it with a forage wagon.

Offers and decisions are preserved when you save your game.

## How to use

1. Accept a mowing contract and close the Contracts menu.
2. If the contract has a grass offer, a confirmation dialog will appear.
3. Accept the reduced reward to keep the grass, or decline to retain the normal contract.
4. Collect everything you want **before collecting payment**.

Your decision is final for that contract. Collection access continues after successful mowing completion, but ends when you collect payment, cancel or fail the contract.

Already-running contracts do not receive new offers retroactively. No additional hotkeys are required.

## Installation

Download **FS25_MowingContractsPlus.zip** from this repository’s Releases section.

Place the ZIP in your active Farming Simulator 25 mods folder **without extracting it**, then enable the mod when loading your savegame.

Before replacing an installed copy, save your game, exit FS25 and back up your savegame. Keep only one installed copy.

## Compatibility

- Single-player only; multiplayer is not supported.
- Compatibility with every map, modified pickup system or other contract mod is not guaranteed.
